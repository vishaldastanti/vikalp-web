Help & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries regarding our clinic assessments, equipment, and home rehabilitation.

Do I need a doctor's prescription before starting physiotherapy?
No, a prior doctor's prescription is not mandatory for your initial diagnostic consultation. Our certified physiotherapists conduct an independent orthopedic and neurological exam. If you already possess MRI scans, X-rays, or surgery discharge summaries, please bring them along.
What equipment is brought for Doorstep Home Visits?
Our therapist arrives equipped with portable clinical electrotherapy units (IFT, TENS, Deep Therapeutic Ultrasound), resistance bands, and mobilization accessories. The 45-minute home session matches the exact therapeutic standard of our clinic.
How many sessions are typically required for recovery?
Acute conditions like neck spasms or mild joint sprains frequently show significant improvement in 3 to 5 sessions. Chronic conditions like slip disc, severe sciatica, or stroke rehabilitation typically require 10 to 15 sessions for full muscle retraining.
What localities in Bihar do you cover?
Our clinic is situated on Main Road, Hajipur (Vaishali). We provide doorstep home visits throughout Hajipur and all major sectors across Greater Patna.
